Your automations can now keep Carerix up to date without a separate step for every kind of change. Adding, updating, archiving and restoring work the same way for candidates, contacts, companies, jobs and more.

<Note>
  This is part of the HireData beta and works in the [new automation builder](/automations/engine-1-3). See [How the Beta Works](/beta/how-the-beta-works) to join.
</Note>

## Keep Carerix up to date from your automations

In a new automation, click **\+**. You find **Add** and **Update** under **Records**, and **Archive**, **Restore** and **Delete** under **Lifecycle**. Then pick the Carerix record you want to change.

### 1. Add new records

Create **candidates**, **contacts** linked to their company, **companies**, **jobs** linked to a contact, **publications** and **meetings** with their attendees. The required fields match the Carerix forms you already know, such as the company on a new job. Publications keep their own text, so Carerix doesn't copy the job text over it.

### 2. Update what's already there

Change any field, or clear it completely, including owners. Keep a candidate's **job title**, **education**, **work history**, **skills** and **languages** up to date, or add them to a **talent pool** without losing the pools they're already in.

### 3. Archive and restore

**Archive** a record when it's no longer active and **Restore** it when you need it again. Only **matches** can be deleted for good, because they're the only records Carerix truly deletes.

### 4. Change several records at once

One step can change several records, such as every open match of a candidate. Add **conditions** so the step only makes the change when the record's current values allow it.

## What else to know

- **Every change is checked after it's saved.** A step can't report success when nothing changed. When Carerix refuses a change, the run shows Carerix's own reason.
- **Fields and labels follow your own Carerix screens**, such as Job instead of Vacancy.
- **Files** can be added to a record from a link, and removed by file name.
- **Not included yet:** meeting visibility, and some talent pool filters and status options.
- **Existing automations keep their old Carerix steps**, such as update field and add note.

## Check it out yourself

Create a new automation in [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations), click **\+**, choose **Add** under **Records** and pick a Carerix record.
