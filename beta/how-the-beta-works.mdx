The HireData beta gives your team early access to new features before they reach everyone. You try them in your own workspace, and your feedback shapes the final version.

## What's in the beta

- **[The new automation builder](/automations/engine-1-3)**: every step sits in a clear section, so you find the right one faster.
- **More ways to change records**: add, update, archive and restore records in your ATS from an automation, including new Carerix records such as candidates, contacts, companies and jobs.
- **Leads and opportunities**: start and run sales workflows with Carerix leads and opportunities.
- **More candidate details**: keep education, skills and languages up to date in Carerix from an automation.
- **[Reviews](/automations/reviews)**: let a colleague approve a step before the automation continues.
- **HireData people**: add, update and look up people in HireData from an automation, with [Find](/automations/look-up-records).

## How it works for your team

### 1. Ask to join

Ask your HireData contact to add your workspace. Access is set per workspace, so your other workspaces stay as they are. Some features, such as Reviews, are switched on separately: your contact can tell you what's active for you.

### 2. Your current automations keep running

Joining the beta doesn't change anything you already built. Existing automations keep their steps and the previous builder. Start a new automation to try the beta features, and contact us before you move an existing one.

### 3. Test with care

A test can send real messages and change real records. Before you share a beta automation with your team:

1. Use test candidates and recipients you're happy to message.
2. Read through each step before you run the test.
3. Check the result in the run history and in your ATS.

You need edit rights on an automation to test it.

### 4. Tell us what you think

Send your HireData contact the workspace, a link to the automation or run, what you expected, what happened and a screenshot. That gets you the fastest answer.

## Leaving the beta

Contact us before you leave, especially if you have beta automations running or reviews waiting for a decision. When Reviews is switched off, waiting decisions are paused until it's switched on again.

## Check it out yourself

Open [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations), create a new automation and click **\+** to see what's new.
