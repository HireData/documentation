Every month we list what changed in HireData, in a few lines per update. Follow the link to the help article to set it up.

<Update label="November 2026" tags={["AI", "Automations"]}>
  - **Score, summarise and sort candidates with Ask AI.** Give every new match a fit score or a short summary with strengths, gaps and red flags. The automation can then match the candidate, decline them or invite them to a pre-screening based on the answer. [Read the guide](/knowledge-base/cookbook/set-up-ask-ai)
  - **ATS values in the candidate's language.** Values from your ATS, such as a job's sector or workplace type, now appear in the language of the form, WhatsApp message or email they're used in. [Read the guide](/settings/setting-a-default-language-and-ai-tone-of-voice)
  - **The right HireData role for Carerix custom roles.** Users with a custom role in Carerix now get the matching Admin or User role when they sync, and the Users page shows their Carerix role next to it. [Read the guide](/apps/carerix/missing-users-after-connecting-carerix)
  - **Spott is now a HireData app.** Connect Spott once and changes in Spott start your automations: message candidates when an application moves stage, check in after a placement starts, and keep each pipeline track apart. [Read the guide](/apps/spott/overview)
  - **Write back to Spott from your automations.** Add notes, update candidates or move applications to another stage in Spott, preview every change before it's saved and see exactly what changed in each run. [Read the guide](/apps/spott/add-update-or-delete-spott-records)
  - **Attach files to automated emails.** The Send Email task can now attach a candidate's latest CV, a file from a web address or a file from your media library, and each run shows which files went out. [Read the guide](/apps/messaging/emails/send-a-cv-as-an-email-attachment)
  - **See where your database can do more with Scan.** One report on how reachable and up to date your Carerix candidates are, what your team achieves, and which automations would close the gaps. [Read the guide](/get-started/read-your-database-scan)
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" tags={["WhatsApp", "Forms"]}>
  - **Pre-screen every form response automatically.** Every completed form is checked against your knock-out questions, the answers are scored, and your recruiter gets a summary. Automations can act on the result straight away. [Read the guide](/knowledge-base/cookbook/set-up-form-evaluations)
  - **Decide what happens when a candidate doesn't reply on WhatsApp.** Each WhatsApp step now has settings for when a candidate doesn't reply, can't be messaged or the message fails: stop the run, or carry on so a later step can follow up. [Read the guide](/apps/messaging/whatsapp/what-happens-when-a-candidate-doesnt-reply)
  - **Filter the WhatsApp inbox.** Filter chats by automated or manual, by who they're assigned to, by WhatsApp number or automation, and by whether they need a reply, so recruiters find the chats that need them. [Read the guide](/apps/messaging/whatsapp/whatsapp-inbox-basics)
</Update>
