Every month we list what changed in HireData, in a few lines per update. Follow the link to the help article to set it up.

<Update label="November 2026" tags={["AI", "Automations"]}>
  - **Score, summarise and sort candidates with Ask AI.** Give every new match a fit score or a short summary with strengths, gaps and red flags. The automation can then match the candidate, decline them or invite them to a pre-screening based on the answer. [Read the guide](/knowledge-base/cookbook/set-up-ask-ai)
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" tags={["WhatsApp", "Forms"]}>
  - **Pre-screen every form response automatically.** Every completed form is checked against your knock-out questions, the answers are scored, and your recruiter gets a summary. Automations can act on the result straight away. [Read the guide](/knowledge-base/cookbook/set-up-form-evaluations)
  - **Decide what happens when a candidate doesn't reply on WhatsApp.** Each WhatsApp step now has settings for when a candidate doesn't reply, can't be messaged or the message fails: stop the run, or carry on so a later step can follow up. [Read the guide](/apps/messaging/whatsapp/what-happens-when-a-candidate-doesnt-reply)
  - **Filter the WhatsApp inbox.** Filter chats by automated or manual, by who they're assigned to, by WhatsApp number or automation, and by whether they need a reply, so recruiters find the chats that need them. [Read the guide](/apps/messaging/whatsapp/whatsapp-inbox-basics)
</Update>
