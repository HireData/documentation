Ask your colleague to send you a link to one item they can open. Open it with your own account in the same HireData workspace. What happens next helps you decide where to look.

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The link opens, but the list looks empty | Open the same list as your colleague and compare any search text or filters. Keep the link so you can report the list problem without recreating the item. |
| Your colleague can open the link and you can’t | Ask a workspace admin to check your sign-in email and role under **Settings > Users**. If a role change disappears after a sync, check the user's **Enable Third-Party Sync** setting. |
| Neither of you can find items you could previously open | Ask an admin to try one known link in the same workspace. Record whether the link opens and whether forms, templates, or automations are affected. |
| You can open a vacancy but can’t configure its questions | Compare the same vacancy and form with your colleague. For per-vacancy configuration, **Vacancy** must be the first object on the form’s **Objects** panel. |

## If the role looks correct

If the same email belongs to an active and an archived Carerix user, ask support to check which user is linked to your HireData account. An archived identity has prevented a recruiter from editing questions even when they could edit the vacancy itself.

Send support the workspace name, affected user's sign-in email, item link, and what a colleague sees at the same link. For a vacancy-specific issue, include the vacancy ID.
