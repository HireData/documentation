Open the affected number in Meta’s **WhatsApp Manager** and its connection in HireData under **Settings > Apps > WhatsApp**. Match the number and WhatsApp Business Account in both places. A workspace can have more than one connection.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The **display name** says **In review** or **Pending** | Check the review for that name in Meta. HireData can show a connected account while the display name is still under review. Record when you submitted it. |
| The **phone number** shows **Offline** | Check that the same number appears on the connected WhatsApp Business Account. Read any registration or account notice Meta displays for that number. |
| You see **Limited** or a payment warning | Open the account notice in Meta to see what is limited. Verify the payment method on the WhatsApp Business Account that owns the number. |
| The settings look correct in Meta and HireData still shows the old status | Open the connection menu in HireData and select **Sync**. Wait for the sync to finish, then reopen the connection. |

## Verify that the number can send

Use an approved template and a test recipient you control. Check the outbound message from **this number** for a delivery result or a provider error. An incoming chat or a successful send from another number leaves this connection untested.

If the status still differs, send support the workspace, WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, exact status from each screen, and the time of the last sync. Include the failed test's message or run ID if you have one.
