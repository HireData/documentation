The **Data** tab is the central place to inspect the information HireData receives from your connected systems and the activity HireData creates around it.

Use it to research a specific record, review a person's communication history, or inspect responses to WhatsApp messages and forms.

## What's in the Data tab

Open **Data** in HireData. At the top of the page, you can switch between three sections:

- **Collections** — Browse and investigate records synced from your connected systems.
- **People** — Review communication with an individual person.
- **Responses** — Inspect answers received through messages and forms.

## Research records in Collections

The **Collections** section contains the objects available from your connected systems. The exact collections depend on the integrations configured for your workspace.

1. Open **Data** and select **Collections**.
2. Choose the collection you want to inspect, such as candidates.
3. Search for a record by its ID. For Carerix records, use the same candidate ID you see in Carerix.
4. Open the record to review its related information.

Depending on the record, you can inspect related people, automations, forms, events, runs, tasks, and synced fields. This makes Collections useful when you want to understand what has happened to a particular candidate or other object.

## Review communication in People

Use **People** to inspect the messages associated with an individual.

1. Open **Data** and select **People**.
2. Find and open the person you want to review.
3. Check their WhatsApp conversations and email messages.

If HireData has not recorded any messages for that person, the conversation history will be empty.

## Inspect answers in Responses

The **Responses** section shows replies received through WhatsApp messages and forms sent by email.

1. Open **Data** and select **Responses**.
2. Open the response you want to investigate.
3. Review when it started, how long completion took, and the submitted answers.
4. When available, follow the links to the related task, event, or automation for more context.

## When to use the Data tab

The Data tab is especially useful when you need to:

- Research what happened to a candidate or another synced record.
- Confirm which automations, runs, tasks, forms, or events relate to a record.
- Review a person's WhatsApp and email history.
- Investigate the source and context of a submitted response.
