_When you send an email in HireData, you can add **CC** and **BCC** recipients so other people receive a copy. You add them on the **Settings** tab of the Send Email task. Follow the steps below._

<Note>
  **CC vs BCC:** CC (carbon copy) recipients are visible to everyone on the email. BCC (blind carbon copy) recipients are hidden from the other recipients.
</Note>

## Step 1: Open the Settings tab

In the **Send Email** task, go to **Step 6 – Preview your email** and select the **Settings** tab. You will see the **CC** and **BCC** sections in the list.

<Frame caption="Replace with Screenshot 1: Step 6 – Settings tab showing the CC and BCC dropdowns">
  ![Step 6 Preview your email, Settings tab with CC and BCC](https://placehold.co/1200x800/eef2f6/64748b?text=Screenshot+1%0AStep+6+Settings+tab+-+CC+%26+BCC)
</Frame>

## Step 2: Open CC or BCC

Click the **CC** or **BCC** dropdown to expand it.

## Step 3: Add the email address

Each recipient has a **Name** and an **Address** field.

To add a plain email address:

1. Leave the **Name** field empty.
2. In the **Address** field, type the email address.
3. When the **Create "email address"** option appears, click it to add the recipient.

<Frame caption="Replace with Screenshot 2: Address field with a typed email and the Create option below it">
  ![Address field with typed email and Create option](https://placehold.co/1000x420/eef2f6/64748b?text=Screenshot+2%0AAddress+field+%2B+Create+option)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  You can also pick an existing contact from the **Name** dropdown instead of typing an address.
</Tip>

## Step 4: Check the entry

Make sure the **Address** shows the email you added and the **Name** field is left empty (unless you selected a contact). Use the **–** button next to an entry to remove it.

<Frame caption="Replace with Screenshot 3: A CC/BCC row with an empty Name, filled Address, and the – remove button">
  ![CC or BCC row with empty Name, filled Address and remove button](https://placehold.co/1000x520/eef2f6/64748b?text=Screenshot+3%0AName+empty%2C+Address+filled%2C+-+button)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Make sure the input field is empty after adding a recipient. An address you typed but did not click **Create** on will **not** be added to the email.
</Warning>

Repeat these steps to add more CC or BCC recipients.
