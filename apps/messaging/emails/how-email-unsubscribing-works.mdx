Email unsubscribing lets recipients stop emails sent to their address from your HireData workspace.

## How recipients unsubscribe

The unsubscribe link in the email body uses two steps:

1. The recipient clicks the unsubscribe link to open the unsubscribe page.
2. They confirm that they want to unsubscribe.

Opening the page alone does not unsubscribe the recipient. This also prevents an email security scanner from unsubscribing someone just by opening the link.

After confirmation, the page shows that the recipient is unsubscribed. They can optionally give a reason and save their feedback. Giving a reason is not required for the unsubscribe to take effect.

<Note>
Some email apps offer their own **Unsubscribe** action beside the sender's name. This uses a separate one-click mechanism and may unsubscribe the recipient without opening the confirmation page.
</Note>

## Which emails does an unsubscribe stop?

The unsubscribe applies to the recipient's email address across the HireData workspace. It is not limited to the newsletter, automation, email brand, or sending domain that they unsubscribed from.

For example, if one workspace sends emails from both `example.com` and `careers-example.com`, unsubscribing from an email sent through either domain stops further automation emails to that address from the same workspace.

There is currently no recipient preference centre to choose individual newsletters, brands, or categories of email. An unsubscribe in one workspace does not by itself unsubscribe the address in a separate workspace.

This controls emails sent through HireData. It does not automatically describe subscription settings in your ATS or another email platform.

## What does the unsubscribe page look like?

The page uses the workspace's name and its default brand's email logo and theme. If no email logo is available, it uses the HireData logo.

Choosing another brand for an individual email, or sending from another verified domain, does not give that email a separate unsubscribe page with its own name, logo, or wording.

### Can I change the text?

The unsubscribe page uses standard HireData text. You cannot currently replace it with your own wording or tone of voice.

You can edit the text around the unsubscribe link in your [email template](/apps/messaging/emails/setting-up-an-email-template-in-hiredata), while keeping the unsubscribe link intact. This does not change the text on the page the link opens.

### Which language is shown?

New unsubscribe links carry the email's language. The page uses that language when supported. Older links without a language, or links with an unsupported language, use the page's normal language fallback.

## Where can I view unsubscribes?

Open **Settings → Unsubscribes**. The list shows the address, date, category, and reason where available. You can filter by category.

The list can also contain addresses suppressed for other reasons, such as an invalid address or a spam report. Not every entry means the recipient used the unsubscribe page.

If an automation tries to email an unsubscribed address, its Send Email task stops with **Email unsubscribed.** See [Send Email task](/reference/automations/tasks/send-email).

To manually prevent emails to an address, use the **Block list** in Settings.

## Can a recipient subscribe again?

A recipient can reopen the unsubscribe link and use the option to subscribe again. Saving feedback alone does not subscribe them again.

The **Unsubscribes** list does not offer a manual resubscribe action for workspace users.
