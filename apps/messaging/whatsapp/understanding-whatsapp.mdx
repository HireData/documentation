WhatsApp offers different products for individuals and businesses. Customers always use the familiar WhatsApp interface, but the way a business manages their messages can differ significantly.

## The different types of WhatsApp

  <Card title="WhatsApp Business App">

### WhatsApp Business App

The WhatsApp Business App adds business features such as a company profile, greeting messages, quick replies, labels, and a product catalogue.

We do not offer or integrate with the WhatsApp Business App. It is included here because it is often confused with the WhatsApp Business Platform.

### WhatsApp Business Platform

The **WhatsApp Business Platform**, also known as the **WhatsApp API** or **Cloud API**, allows companies to connect WhatsApp to professional business software.

Businesses can use it for:

- Automated notifications and reminders
- Customer service conversations
- Recruitment and candidate communication
- Marketing campaigns
- Chatbots and AI assistants
- Shared access for multiple employees
- Connections with a CRM, ATS, or automation platform

<Tip>
  The WhatsApp Business App is operated manually on a phone. The WhatsApp Business Platform connects WhatsApp to business software and automations.
</Tip>

## How a WhatsApp setup fits together

A typical WhatsApp Business Platform setup contains several connected parts:

```text
Meta Business Portfolio
└── WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)
    ├── Business phone number
    │   ├── Display name
    │   ├── Business profile
    │   └── Phone Number ID
    └── Message templates
```

Each part has its own purpose and, in many cases, its own ID.

## Accounts and IDs

### Meta Business Portfolio

A **Meta Business Portfolio**, previously called a **Business Manager account**, is the main environment in which a company manages its Meta assets.

It can contain Facebook Pages, advertising accounts, users, and WhatsApp Business Accounts.

The **Business Portfolio ID**, also called the **Business ID** or **Business Manager ID**, uniquely identifies this environment.

### WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)

A **WhatsApp Business Account**, usually abbreviated to **WABA**, is the administrative account containing a company's WhatsApp Business Platform assets.

A WABA can contain business phone numbers, message templates, account settings, quality information, and payment information.

The **WABA ID** identifies the account as a whole. It does not identify an individual phone number.

### Business phone number

The business phone number is the real number customers see and contact on WhatsApp—for example, `+31 20 123 4567`.

The business must prove that it controls this number during setup.

### Phone Number ID

The **Phone Number ID** is an internal Meta identifier assigned to a business phone number.

For example:

- Telephone number customers see: `+31 20 123 4567`
- Internal Phone Number ID: `107654321234567`

The WhatsApp API uses the Phone Number ID to determine which business number should send a message. It is not interchangeable with the actual telephone number.

### Display name

The **display name** is the business name customers see in their WhatsApp conversation—for example, “Example Recruitment.”

Meta reviews this name. It must accurately represent the business and comply with WhatsApp's naming rules.

Display-name approval is separate from verifying control of the phone number, completing Meta business verification, and receiving a verified badge.

### WhatsApp ID (`wa_id`)

A **WhatsApp ID**, commonly shown as `wa_id`, identifies a person on WhatsApp.

It is normally based on the person's international phone number without spaces or a plus sign. For example, `31612345678`.

- A **Phone Number ID** identifies the company's sending number inside Meta.
- A **`wa_id`** identifies a WhatsApp user or recipient.

### Meta App ID

A **Meta App** provides the technical connection between WhatsApp and another software platform.

The **App ID** identifies that application. It does not identify the company, its WABA, or its phone number.

### Access token

An **access token** is a secret credential that allows software to access the WhatsApp API.

<Warning>
  Treat an access token like a password. Never include it in screenshots, support tickets, public documents, or source code.
</Warning>

### Message ID

Every sent and received message has a unique **Message ID**. WhatsApp message IDs often begin with `wamid`.

A system uses this ID to match replies to earlier messages, mark incoming messages as read, track delivery, and determine whether a message was sent, delivered, read, or failed.

### Template ID and template name

A **message template** is a reusable message format reviewed by WhatsApp.

Each template has a name, language, category, review status, Template ID, and content with optional variables and buttons.

The template name is normally used when sending the message. The numerical Template ID identifies the template inside Meta.

## Important messaging terms

### The 24-hour customer service window

When a person sends a message to a business, a 24-hour customer service window opens. Every new message from that person restarts the window.

During this window, the business can normally send free-form service responses. Outside it, an approved message template is generally required to restart the conversation.

### Message templates

A **message template** is used when a business initiates a conversation or sends a message outside the 24-hour customer service window.

Templates are submitted to WhatsApp for review. They can contain fixed text, variables, media, and buttons.

A variable represents information that changes for each recipient:

```text Example template
Hi {{1}}, your interview is scheduled for {{2}} at {{3}}.
```

The system could replace these variables with a candidate's name, interview date, and interview time.

### Embedded Signup

**Embedded Signup** is Meta's guided process for connecting a company's WhatsApp account to a software platform.

During this process, the customer signs in to Meta and selects or creates:

- A Meta Business Portfolio
- A WhatsApp Business Account
- A business phone number
- The permissions required by the connected software

## IDs at a glance

| ID | What it identifies |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Portfolio ID** | The company's overall business environment in Meta |
| **WABA ID** | The company's WhatsApp Business Account |
| **Phone Number ID** | A business sending number inside the API |
| **WhatsApp ID** | A WhatsApp user or recipient |
| **App ID** | The application connected to WhatsApp |
| **Template ID** | An approved message template |
| **Message ID** | One individual sent or received message |

<Note>
  Many of these IDs are long numbers and can look similar. Each belongs to a different part of the setup, so they cannot be used interchangeably.
</Note>
