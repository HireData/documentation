_Welcome to our manual on syncing your Carerix account with HireData. Please follow the instructions below to manually sync objects, statuses, and users — this is how you refresh or update your Carerix data on demand. This way, you can use a candidate status, custom field or match stage you've just created or updated within Carerix, without waiting for the daily sync between your accounts._

To do that, you should first [connect your Carerix](/apps/carerix/connect-with-carerix) account with HireData.

We will automatically schedule a sync to import the users' data from Carerix to HireData once the connection is established. That is required so the users can automatically send different types of communication through our platform. Then we schedule daily resyncs in case you make any changes in your Carerix profile.

However, you can also manually sync when, for instance, you want to see the changes you have made in your Carerix environment reflected in your HireData profile right away. Here is how to do it:

## Step 1: Navigate to the Settings page

Sign in to HireData and select **Settings** in the main navigation.

## Step 2: Open the Apps menu

Select **Apps**.

## Step 3: Select the Carerix app

Open the **Carerix** app by selecting its name or **View App**.

## Step 4: Sync the data

Open the three-dot menu for your Carerix connection and select **Sync**. HireData schedules the sync and shows its progress.

## Pro tip

The **Data Sync Timeline** on the Carerix app page shows what was synced and whether the sync completed.
