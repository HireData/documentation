Follow the instructions below to manually sync objects, statuses, and users between your Carerix account and HireData. This lets you use a candidate status, custom field, or match stage you just created or updated in Carerix, without waiting for the daily sync.

To do that, you should first [connect your Carerix](/apps/carerix/connect-with-carerix) account with HireData.

After you connect your account, HireData automatically schedules a sync to import your users' data from Carerix. HireData needs this data so your users can automatically send different types of communication through the platform. HireData then schedules daily resyncs in case you make any changes in your Carerix profile.

You can also sync manually. For example, sync manually when you want changes from your Carerix environment to appear in your HireData profile right away. Here is how to do it:

## Step 1: Navigate to the Settings page

Log in to your HireData account and open the Settings page via the **Settings** button above your name.

![Settings button](/images/intercom/3cde4c7febdd.png)

## Step 2: Open the Apps menu

![Apps menu](/images/intercom/0418411d95ee.png)

## Step 3: Select the Carerix app

You can open the Carerix app either by clicking the name or the **View app** button.

![View app button](/images/intercom/5c8a51ef1364.png)

## Step 4: Sync the data

Click the **Sync** button (the two arrows in a circular motion) located at the end of the company's connection. After you click it, HireData shows the sync progress.

![](/images/intercom/a7a4d05ef245.png)

## Pro tip

When you click the **Timeline** button (the first of the four buttons), you can see what has been synced.

![Data sync timeline](/images/intercom/31cd3f5e5d7c.png)
