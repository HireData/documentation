Manually sync your OTYS account with HireData to update objects, statuses, and users on demand. This lets you use a newly created or updated candidate status, custom field, or match stage without waiting for the daily sync.

To do that, you should first [connect your OTYS](/apps/otys/connect-with-otys) account with HireData.

Once the connection is established, HireData automatically schedules a sync to import the users' data from OTYS. That is required so the users can automatically send different types of communication through the platform. HireData then schedules daily resyncs in case you make any changes in your OTYS profile.

However, you can also manually sync when, for instance, you want to see the changes you have made in your OTYS environment reflected in your HireData profile immediately. Here is how to do it:

## Step 1: Navigate to the Settings page

Log in to your HireData account and open the Settings page via the [Settings] button above your name.

![Settings button](/images/intercom/49935101-image.png)

## Step 2: Open the Apps menu

![Apps page in HireData](/images/intercom/49935163-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-11_18_28.png)

## Step 3: Select the OTYS app

You can open the Vincere app either by clicking the name or the [View app] button.

![View app button](/images/intercom/49935188-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-11_18_28-copy.png)

## Step 4: Sync the data

Click the [Sync] button located in the drop-down menu of the company's connection.

![Sync button](/images/intercom/49935249-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-11_25_45.png)

After you have clicked it, you will see the syncing progress.

![Syncing progress](/images/intercom/49935294-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-11_25_53.png)

Finally, when the sync has finished successfully, you should receive this message at the bottom of the Data Sync Timeline:

![Data Sync Timeline](/images/intercom/49935331-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-11_26_21.png)
