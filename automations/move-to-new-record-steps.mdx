Engine 1.3.0 brings one set of record steps to add, update, archive and restore records in Carerix and HireData. This FAQ explains what changes for your existing automations and what you may need to check.

<Note>
  Engine 1.3.0 is available for beta accounts. Other accounts follow later.
</Note>

## What changes for your automations

### 1. Do my live automations change?

No. Every existing automation keeps running unchanged on its current engine, with its current tasks and the previous step drawer.

### 2. Which automations use engine 1.3.0?

**New automations** in a beta account start on engine 1.3.0. Only beta accounts can choose engine 1.3.0 for an automation.

### 3. What happens to my existing Carerix steps?

Existing Carerix write steps can be moved to the new record steps, per account:

- **Update Object** steps move to the new **Update** step.
- **Notes, tasks and e-mails** move to the new record steps.

We move beta accounts first, one account at a time. Only automations without engine warnings are moved.

### 4. What if a step can't move fully?

Steps that can't move fully are listed with a reason, so they can be checked by hand before anything changes.

## What else changed in the builder

- **Prompt is now called Ask AI.**
- **The step drawer** groups steps into clear sections, from Flow to Lifecycle.
- **Post Webhook, Search Domain, Verify Email, Find Email and Sync Person** are no longer offered for new steps. Existing steps with these tasks keep running.

## Check it out yourself

Create a new automation in [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations) and open the step drawer to see the new record steps. Questions about your account's move? Contact [support@hiredata.com](mailto:support@hiredata.com).
