New automations in beta accounts now run on engine 1.3. It brings one set of record steps for Carerix and HireData, and a step drawer grouped into clear sections. Your existing automations keep running exactly as they do today.

<Note>
  Engine 1.3 is available for beta accounts. In other accounts, new automations are built on engine 1.2.
</Note>

## What changes for your automations

### 1. New automations start on engine 1.3

In a beta account, every new automation in [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations) starts on engine 1.3. Only beta accounts can choose engine 1.3.

### 2. Existing automations don't change

Automations you already have keep running on their current engine, with their current steps and the previous step drawer.

![The top of the new step drawer, with the Flow, Search and Outreach sections](/images/automations/step-drawer-top.webp)

### 3. New Carerix record steps

On engine 1.3, general steps create, update, delete, archive and restore a Carerix record. They replace the old Carerix steps **update field**, **add note**, **add task**, **add email** and **add publication**. See [Create, update and archive Carerix records](/automations/carerix-record-steps).

![The Records, AI and tools, Human in the loop and Lifecycle sections of the step drawer](/images/automations/step-drawer-records-lifecycle.webp)

### 4. Steps no longer offered

**Find Email**, **Verify Email**, **Search Domain**, **Sync Person** and **Post Webhook** are no longer offered in new automations. Existing steps with these tasks keep running.

## What else changed?

- **The step drawer** groups steps into sections: Flow, Search, Outreach, Records, AI and tools, Human in the loop and Lifecycle.
- **Prompt is now called Ask AI.**
- **HTTP request** replaces Post Webhook for calling another web service.

## Check it out yourself

Create a new automation in [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations) and click **+** to open the new step drawer.
