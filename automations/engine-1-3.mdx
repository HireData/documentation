Building an automation now works like picking from a tidy toolbox. When you add a step, every option sits in a section that says what it does, so you find what you need without knowing its technical name.

<Note>
  The new automation builder is part of the HireData beta. Ask your HireData contact to add your workspace. Your existing automations keep working exactly as they do today.
</Note>

## Find the right step faster

Open a new automation in [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations) and click **\+**. The steps are grouped by what they do for you.

![The step list in the new automation builder, with the Flow, Search and Outreach sections](/images/automations/step-drawer-top.webp)

### 1. Steps grouped by what they do

- **Flow**: decide who continues (Filter), take different paths (Split), wait (Delay) or repeat steps (Loop).
- **Search**: look up a candidate, contact or other record with **Find**.
- **Outreach**: send an **Email**, a **WhatsApp message**, a **WhatsApp survey** or a **Ratecard survey**. WhatsApp options show once WhatsApp is connected.
- **Records**: **Add** or **Update** a record in your ATS or in HireData.
- **AI and tools**: **Ask AI** to write, sort or pick out information, or send an **HTTP request** to another system.
- **Human in the loop**: let a colleague approve a step first with a [Review](/automations/reviews).
- **Lifecycle**: **Archive**, **Restore** or **Delete** a record.

### 2. One way to change any record

Adding, updating, archiving and restoring records now works the same in every connected app. In Carerix you can, for example, add candidates, contacts, companies and jobs, or update a candidate's education and skills.

### 3. Your current automations stay as they are

Automations you already built keep their steps and the previous builder. Joining the beta doesn't move them. Start a new automation to try the new builder, and contact us before you move an existing one.

## What else changed?

- **Prompt is now called Ask AI.**
- **Some older steps aren't offered in new automations**: Find Email, Verify Email, Search Domain, Sync Person (replaced by the HireData people steps) and Post Webhook (replaced by HTTP request). Automations that already use them keep running.
- **Testing an automation needs edit rights**, because a test can send messages and change records.

## Check it out yourself

Create a new automation in [Automations](https://app.hiredata.com/automations) and click **\+** to see the new list of steps.
