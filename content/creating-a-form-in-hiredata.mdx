_The forms in HireData help you collect structured data during candidate journeys. This guide walks you through setting up and using forms in HireData for your automations._

## Creating a form

First, **define the purpose of the form.** Decide what data you need to collect, such as a candidate's availability or feedback on the recruitment process.

Then open the **Forms** page from **Settings** and click **New Form**. You can create a blank form or pick one of the templates:

![New Form button](/images/intercom/50372488-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-09_38_44.png)

_Form templates:_

![Form templates](/images/intercom/50372790-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-09_39_14.png)

_If you start from scratch, give the form a name. The name is for internal use only:_

![Adding a name to a form](/images/intercom/50373594-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-12_40_06.png)

## Adding questions

A form is one or more questions. Each question can carry logic, for example to show a follow-up only when a rating is below 8. Click the **+** button above the question list to open the **Add Content** palette and pick a question type.

The palette offers text, contact, date and time, choice, numeric, rating, file upload, and ending types. For what each type stores and the logic operators it supports, see the [form builder overview](/reference/forms/overview).

![Question types](/images/intercom/50373968-Screenshot-2025-03-19-at-21_07_17.png)

### Question settings

Click a question to open its settings. Every question has a **Description** and an **Image**. Text questions add a **Placeholder**, rating questions a scale and a **Reverse** toggle, and choice questions their options. The settings that every field carries are in [Settings every field shares](/reference/forms/overview#settings-every-field-shares).

The button at the bottom right of the card opens **Display & requirements**, which holds three switches:

- **Hidden** keeps the question out of the form while its stored answer stays available to your automations.
- **Required** stops the respondent from continuing until they answer.
- **Always include** stops the question being switched off for an individual record, and makes it required.

See [Display and requirements](/reference/forms/overview#display-and-requirements) for how the three work together.

![Adding a question to a form](/images/intercom/50374651-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-09_48_54.png)

<Tip>
  Drag and drop to reorder the questions.
</Tip>

Before you make a Yes/No or numeric question required, read [What counts as an answer](/reference/forms/overview#required-answers).

## Adding logic to the questions

Use **rules** to set up flows between questions. For example:

- If a candidate rates **7 or below**, show a follow-up asking for improvement suggestions.
- If the rating is **8 or higher**, take them to the next question or ask them to leave a Google review.

To set this up, open the **Logic** tab and click **Add Rule** under a question. Set the condition, for example "is less than or equal to 7", then choose the question or ending screen to go to. **All other cases go to** decides where everyone else continues.

![Logic tab](/images/intercom/50376095-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-12_54_56.png)

Which operators each question type supports and how HireData picks between several rules are in the [Logic tab reference](/reference/forms/logic).

## Adding a thank-you screen

Based on the answers, forms can end with a thank-you message or redirect candidates to an external page to leave a review.

You can customise the **Thank-you screen** with a:

- Title
- Short message
- Image
- Button with text
- Redirect URL. Use this to direct candidates to leave a review or go to your website.

## Preview and save the form

Click the eye icon to preview the form and **Save** to save it.

![Save and Preview Buttons](/images/intercom/50379428-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-13_18_51.png)

_Form preview on different devices:_

![Form preview](/images/intercom/50379844-Screenshot-2025-05-19-at-13_21_09.png)

Once your form is ready, add it to your email templates in HireData and send it through your automations.

## Tailor the form per record

Link the form to an object such as a vacancy, and you can switch individual questions on or off for one record without touching the form itself. One pre-screening form then covers every vacancy, each serving only the questions that belong to it. See [Configuring a form per record](/content/configuring-a-form-per-record).
